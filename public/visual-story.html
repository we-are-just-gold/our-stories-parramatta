My day at

Our Stories.

What I will see and do at PHIVE on Saturday 28 November 2026. One step at a time, in pictures.

Start at step 1What the day may feel like

  1. 1 of 13. Saturday 28 November 2026

    I am going to Our Stories

    Our Stories is a day of hearing stories and telling stories.

    It is at PHIVE, in Parramatta.

    • I can come to one session, two or all three

  2. 2 of 13. Getting there

    I travel to PHIVE

    PHIVE is at 5 Parramatta Square. It is the building with the red roof, next to Parramatta Town Hall.

    It is a two minute walk from Parramatta station.

    Directions by train, light rail, bus and car

    Train. I tap off and turn right at the Parramatta Square sign. Harvey’s Hot Sandwiches is on my left. I walk straight to the silver bus sculpture, then turn left. PHIVE is the building with the red roof.

    Light rail. From the Macquarie Street stop I turn right and walk about 50 metres. I turn left into the first laneway, with the NAB sign on my left. I walk straight. PHIVE is on my right.

    Bus. From the Argyle Street stops I find the Huss Newsstand. I take the escalators or the stairs down and turn left. Then I follow the train directions.

    Car. There is a drop off zone in front of the entrance doors. The Parramatta Square Carpark entrance ramp is on Darcy Street, just after the corner of Smith Street. It is paid, with no ticket and no cash. Other car parks are in the welcome pack.

  3. 3 of 13. Going in

    I go in the main entrance

    I walk in from Parramatta Square, through the large glass doors or the automatic sliding door.

    Now I am in the foyer on the Ground Floor. The Customer Service desk is on my right.

    • The foyer can be busy

  4. 4 of 13. Going up

    I take the lift or the stairs to Level 4

    Our Stories is in the Civic Gallery, on Level 4.

    The stairs are through the double glass doors beside the lifts.

    • Lifts can be crowded. People might bump into me

  5. 5 of 13. From 9.30am

    I sign in at the welcome desk

    The welcome desk is on Level 4. I say my name and I get a name badge.

    Volunteers wear a black shirt and a badge. I can ask them for help.

    • If I have an allergy, I tell the welcome desk

  6. 6 of 13. Photos and filming

    I choose if I am in photos

    People are taking photos and video today.

    If I do not want to be in them, I ask for a bright sticker at the welcome desk. Saying no is easy.

    • Cameras are in the room

    Open the consent form

  7. 7 of 13. When I arrive

    I open the app on my phone

    I point my phone camera at the QR code. Then I tap Start your day.

    It asks 10 short questions. It does not ask my name.

    • No phone? Paper copies are at the welcome desk

    Start your day

  8. 8 of 13. 10am to 12pm

    Hear

    I sit in the Civic Gallery. It starts with a Welcome to Country.

    People talk about belonging in Parramatta. There is a performance. We answer a poll on our phones.

    • Talking and a performance
    • I can just listen

  9. 9 of 13. 12pm to 12.45pm

    Lunch

    Lunch is in the foyer of the Civic Gallery. It is for everyone who comes to any session.

    All the food is halal. There are gluten free, vegetarian and vegan options.

    • Dance, a flute and an electric guitar
    • The smell of food
    • People eating and talking

  10. 10 of 13. 12.45pm to 2.45pm

    Create

    I work on my own story in a workshop.

    People aged 15 to 25 have their own session, with writing and performance.

    • I only share what I want to share
    • I can use my mother tongue

  11. 11 of 13. 3pm to 5pm

    Share

    We sit in small circles. I can tell my story, or I can listen. Listening is taking part.

    Some people tell a story on the stage. That only happens if they choose it.

    • Some stories bring things up. That is normal

  12. 12 of 13. Any time

    I can take a break

    I can leave any session when I need to, and come back when I am ready.

    The quiet space is in the foyer on Level 4. A counsellor is here from about 11am.

    • Quiet space, Level 4
    • Prayer room, Level 3
    • Seats outside, near trees and grass

  13. 13 of 13. Going home

    I leave when I am ready

    Before I go, I tap End your day in the app.

    I take the lift or the stairs to the Ground Floor and walk out through the glass doors. If the sliding door is closed, I press the green button.

    End your day

Things I can bring

  • Headphones or earplugs
  • Fidgets
  • Sunglasses

Places that are calmer

  • Quiet space, Level 4. In the foyer. Open all day.
  • Prayer room, Level 3. On my left as I leave the lift. I press the intercom at the door.
  • Outside. Parramatta Square and Centenary Square have seats near trees and grass.
  • Around PHIVE. There are quiet corners with natural light.

Help in the building

If I need more time or help

Hidden Disabilities Sunflower. PHIVE is Sunflower Friendly. Wearing a Sunflower shows that I may need more time or help. I do not need one to ask for help. Sunflower items are at Reception on the Ground Floor.

Hearing. There is a free hearing app, opened with a QR code. I can also borrow a device from Customer Service on the Ground Floor.

Who to ask. Any volunteer in a black shirt, or the welcome desk on Level 4.

Access

Getting around

Wheelchair access. PHIVE is wheelchair accessible. I can take the lift to Level 4.

Adult changing facility. It has a hoist. It is on the Basement Mezzanine, one level below the Ground Floor. I use the intercom.

Parents room. Level 1, on the left as I leave the lift. I use the intercom.

Drop off. There is a drop off zone in front of the entrance doors, and accessible spaces in the car park.
