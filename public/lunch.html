Lunch

is on us.

Lunch is for everyone attending any session, whether you came for Hear, Create, Share or the whole day.

Lunch is by Racha’s Syrian Kitchen

Bold and authentic Syrian food from traditional recipes, by a lover of good food, storytelling and family gatherings. Racha’s Syrian Kitchen caters events, teaches cooking classes and runs a pop up kitchen.
