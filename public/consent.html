One minute. It tells us how you want to be treated on the day.

Our Stories, Parramatta is funded by the NSW Government to learn what belonging looks like for the people who live, work, study or visit Parramatta.

By taking part today you are taking part in the research. What is said in the room, in the surveys and on this site is captured without names and becomes themes in a report for SydWest, the City of Parramatta and the NSW Government. Once the report is published, what you contributed cannot be taken out of it.

This form is about photos, video and recordings. Saying no changes nothing about how you take part, and you can change your choice on the day by telling a volunteer at the welcome desk.

1. Your name, as on your booking
2. Photos and video of the day

Please ask your parent or guardian to fill this in for you. Nobody under 18 takes part in any session until a parent or guardian has filled this in.
