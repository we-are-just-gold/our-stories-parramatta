Privacy notice

What we collect and

what we do with it.

Plain answers about every form on this site.

The short version. Just Gold collects what you send on this site on behalf of SydWest Multicultural Services. Just Gold keeps the record for one month after Saturday 28 November 2026, then hands it to SydWest and deletes its own copy. Nothing you send is sold, used for marketing or shared with anyone else.

Who is collecting it

Our Stories, Parramatta is an initiative of SydWest and the City of Parramatta, funded by the NSW Government through the NSW Social Cohesion Grants for Local Government program. Just Gold Digital Agency Pty Ltd (Just Gold) runs this site and the evaluation of the day for SydWest.

What each form collects

  • Arrival survey and end of day survey. No name, email or phone number. They ask your age range, some questions about you such as gender, country of birth, language and postcode, and what you think and feel. You can skip any question.
  • Add your voice, the three questions in Hear and your circle’s message in Share. Only what you write, and the session or table if you tell us. No name.
  • Quick fire in Hear. Seven tick questions about your neighbourhood, volunteering, your future and being left out. No name.
  • Check in with yourself in Create. Not sent and not saved. Your answers are for you.
  • A stage spot. Your name and how long you would like, so we can call you up.
  • Read it for me. What you want read from the stage, and a first name only if you add one.
  • Facilitator reflection. The facilitator’s or host’s name, what they heard in the room, and up to five pictures of the work if they add them.
  • Share your story. Your story, and up to five pictures if you add them. Your name and an email or phone number only if you choose to be named.
  • Consent form. Your name, your choice about photos and video, and your typed signature. If you are signing for someone under 18, their name, your name and your mobile number.

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps one small note in your own browser so the buttons can show which steps you have finished. That note stays on your phone.

Why we collect it

To understand what the day was like for the people who came, to report on it to SydWest, the City of Parramatta and the NSW Government, and to respect your choices about photos, video and your story.

How it is used

  • Survey answers and what you add through the day are reported as themes and numbers. In reporting, your responses will not be identifiable in any way.
  • If we quote something you wrote, identifying details are removed.
  • A story is published with a name only if the person who sent it chose to be named. Pictures sent with a story are used only with that story.
  • The consent form is used on the day so the photographer and volunteers know your choice.
  • Once the report is published, what you contributed to it cannot be taken out.

Who sees it

Just Gold and SydWest. The City of Parramatta and the NSW Government receive the report, which carries no names unless a person chose to be named in a story.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • What you send is stored by the company that hosts this site, Netlify, which is based in the United States, until Just Gold exports it.
  • Just Gold keeps the record for one month after Saturday 28 November 2026.
  • Just Gold then hands the record to SydWest and deletes its own copy, including the copy held by the site host.
  • After that, SydWest holds it under the SydWest privacy policy.
  • SydWest and Just Gold will not use photos, video or recordings from the day after five years. The NSW Premier’s Department, which funds the day, may also use them.

Your choices

  • Everything on this site is voluntary. You can take part in the day without filling in any survey.
  • You can change your photo and video choice at any time on the day by telling a volunteer at the welcome desk.
  • If you sent a story or a consent form, you can ask to see it, correct it or withdraw it. Contact us below.
  • The surveys and Add your voice carry no name, so we cannot find or remove one person’s answers after they are sent.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information go to Kyriakos Gold at Just Gold, kyriakos@justgold.net. SydWest can be reached through sydwestms.org.au.
