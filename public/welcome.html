The day at a glance

One day, three sessions

Come to one session, two or all three. Coming just to listen is taking part. Nobody is asked to share anything they do not want to, and you can use your mother tongue.

  1. 9.30amDoors open. Sign in, pick up your name badge and fill in the arrival survey.Welcome desk, Level 4
  2. 10am to 12pmHear. Welcome to Country at 10.05am, conversation, performance and the room’s first poll.Civic Gallery, Level 4
  3. 12pm to 12.45pmLunch for everyone attending any session. Cultural dance, and a conversation between a flautist and an electric guitarist.Civic Gallery foyer
  4. 12.45pm to 2.45pmCreate. A storytelling workshop for adults, and a writing and performance session for ages 15 to 25.Adults: Civic Gallery
  5. 3pm to 5pmShare. Small sharing circles, stage spots for people who opt in, the end of day survey and wrap up.Civic Gallery, Level 4

The venue

PHIVE

5 Parramatta Square, Parramatta NSW 2150. PHIVE is the building with the City of Parramatta Library, next to Parramatta Town Hall.

Train. PHIVE is two minutes on foot from Parramatta station. Leave the station onto Parramatta Square and PHIVE is on your right.

Inside. Use the main entrance on the ground level and take the lift or stairs to Level 4 for the Civic Gallery. PHIVE is wheelchair accessible.

Wi-Fi. Free public Wi-Fi runs through PHIVE. The network is called PARRAMATTA, Where it’s AT.

Open all day

Spaces for you

Quiet space, Level 4. In the foyer. Step out of any session whenever you need to, and come back when you are ready.

Prayer room, Level 3. On your left as you leave the lift. Press the intercom at the door.

Lunch. At 12pm for everyone attending any session, by Racha’s Syrian Kitchen. All food is halal, with gluten free, vegetarian and vegan options.

If you have a nut allergy or any other allergy, tell us at the welcome desk.

Parking. O’Connell Street Car Park, 21 O’Connell Street, is free on weekends and 14 minutes on foot. Westfield Parramatta gives Westfield members four hours free and is 5 minutes on foot. Parramatta Station Car Park, Wentworth Street, and Justice Precinct Car Park, 13 Hunter Street, are $14 for 3 to 18 hours and 7 minutes on foot.

Photos, filming and consent

Your image, your choice

We are taking photos and video. Saying no is easy, and it changes nothing about how you take part.

  • A bright sticker means: do not photograph or film me, wide shots included. Ask for one at the welcome desk.
  • Camera free tables stay out of frame.
  • A stage spot in Share is photographed and filmed, and only happens if you choose one.
  • Bringing someone under 18? Tell us before Saturday. A parent or guardian fills in the consent form for them before they join any session.
  • Nobody is named unless they choose to be.
  • Changed your mind? Tell any volunteer. If you ask for a photo or clip of you to be deleted, it is deleted.
  • Your own phone: ask before you photograph or record anyone’s story.

What happens to what we hear

How we use what we share

By taking part today you are taking part in the research. What is said in the room, in the surveys and on this site is captured without names and becomes themes in a report for SydWest, the City of Parramatta and the NSW Government. Once the report is published, what you contributed cannot be taken out of it.

The arrival and end of day surveys feed the report that goes back to everyone who took part.

Want to tell your story in full? Share your story, with or without your name.

We follow the Transformational Ethical Story Telling (T.E.S.T.) principles from Our Race.

Looking after people

Some stories bring things up

That is normal. Step out to the quiet space whenever you need to. A counsellor is on site from about 11am, and there is a support card on every table. In an emergency call triple zero (000).

  • Lifeline13 11 14. Crisis support, 24 hours. For an interpreter call 13 14 50 first.
  • 13YARN13 92 76. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander crisis support, 24 hours.
  • Transcultural Mental Health Centre1800 648 911. Support in your language. Monday to Friday, 9am to 4.30pm.
  • STARTTS02 9646 6700. Survivors of torture and refugee trauma, and their families.
  • QLife1800 184 527. LGBTIQ+ peer support.
  • Kids Helpline1800 55 1800. Young people aged 5 to 25, 24 hours.
