3pm to 5pm. Civic Gallery, Level 4

Share

in a small circle.

Tell your story in a small circle, or simply listen.

With you in Share

Dr Rumbi Mabambe (Dr Ru)

Your facilitator for the day

A facilitator, researcher and CEO of Chain Reaction Foundation. She brings warmth and curiosity to conversations about what helps people feel heard, connected and at home in their communities.

A host sits at every table.

  1. 1

    First session today? Start your day

    The arrival survey. 10 short questions about you and your community, with no name.

    Start your day

  2. 2

    What happens in Share

    • About 45 minutes in circles. You share and listen at your table, with a host.
    • About half an hour on stage. A performer opens, then anyone who chose a stage spot shares. The stage is photographed and filmed.
    • A web of connection. Back at your table, a ball of wool goes from person to person as you say what you heard from each other that helped you feel connected.
    • Closing reflections and what happens next. We finish by 4.50pm.
  3. 3

    On stage, or read for you

    Want to share on stage?

    Put my name down for a stage spot

    Rather stay in your seat? Write it here and a volunteer or performer reads it from the stage for you.

    What is read from the stage is filmed with the stage. Leave out names and personal details, yours and other people’s. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. If something brings things up, the support numbers are here.

  4. 4

    Your circle’s message

    One message from your circle. Anyone at the table can send it. Leave out names and personal details, yours and other people’s. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. If something brings things up, the support numbers are here.

  5. 5

    More to say?

    Tell your story in full, with or without your name, and add pictures if you like.

    Share your story

  6. 6

    Before you leave

    The end of day survey. About five minutes on what you heard, who you met and what you will carry home.

    End your day

Just Gold holds what you send here on behalf of SydWest. It keeps the record for one month after the day, then hands it to SydWest and deletes its own copy. Read the privacy notice.

Our Stories, Parramatta is an initiative of SydWest and the City of Parramatta, funded by the NSW Government through the NSW Social Cohesion Grants for Local Government program.
