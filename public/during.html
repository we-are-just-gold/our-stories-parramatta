By taking part today you are taking part in the research. What is said in the room, in the surveys and on this site is captured without names and becomes themes in a report for SydWest, the City of Parramatta and the NSW Government. Once the report is published, what you contributed cannot be taken out of it.

Leave out names and personal details, yours and other people’s. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. If something brings things up, the support numbers are here.

Just Gold holds what you send here on behalf of SydWest. It keeps the record for one month after the day, then hands it to SydWest and deletes its own copy. Read the privacy notice.
