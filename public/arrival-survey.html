A few questions about you and your community. Fill it in when you arrive.

The NSW Premier’s Department is evaluating the NSW Social Cohesion Grants for Local Government program, which funds Our Stories, Parramatta. SydWest and the City of Parramatta also use this survey to understand who came and how people feel about their community.

It is voluntary and anonymous. In reporting, your responses will not be identifiable in any way. You can skip any question and still take part in the day.

Questions about the survey go to Kyriakos Gold at Just Gold, kyriakos@justgold.net. The survey is not monitored for urgent help. On the day, speak to any volunteer and they will find the event lead.

If you feel upset and need more support, you can call:

  • Lifeline 13 11 14
  • Beyond Blue Support Service 1300 22 4636
  • Kids Helpline 1800 55 1800, for people aged 5 to 25
  • 13YARN 13 92 76, for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander services

Just Gold holds what you send here on behalf of SydWest. It keeps the record for one month after the day, then hands it to SydWest and deletes its own copy. Read the privacy notice.

By continuing with the survey you are providing consent to participate in this research.

1. What is your age?

Thanks for participating in this survey. Unfortunately, we can only hear from participants 16 years or over in this survey.

2. What is your gender?
3. Are you of Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander origin?
4. What is your country of birth?
5. Were your parents born in Australia?
6. Do you use a language other than English at home?
7. What is your postcode?

Optional.

8. To what extent do you agree with the following statements?

I feel connected to my community

I feel like I belong in my community

I engage regularly with people with different experiences and worldview

I believe I can make a valuable contribution to community activities and initiatives

9. How comfortable do you feel about sharing a story of your own?
10. What do you hope to gain from the day?
