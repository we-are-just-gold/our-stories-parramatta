Shape your own story with Our Race. You decide what you tell, and what you keep.

Create is led by Our Race

Our Race Community is an anti-racism storytelling charity that champions authentic narrative representation. It partners directly with individuals and communities affected by racial and social injustices, amplifying voices that have historically been marginalised. At the heart of its approach is the Transformational Ethical Story Telling (T.E.S.T.) Framework, placing the wellbeing, agency and safety of Story Holders at the centre of how stories are invited, developed and shared.

    What happens in Create

    • Two rooms with Our Race: a storytelling workshop for adults, and a writing and performance session for ages 15 to 25.

    Nobody under 18 joins any session without a parent or guardian’s consent form.

    You are the Story Holder

    A Story Holder is the person who has lived the story. You own it, and you choose whether to share it. Step 3 takes you through the questions to ask yourself first.

    You have the right to decide if, when and how your story is shared. If you decide later that you no longer want to, that is OK too.

    Read the Story Holder guide (PDF)

    The five T.E.S.T. Principles

    Transformational Ethical Story Telling (T.E.S.T.) is Our Race’s framework for keeping the Story Holder at the centre. Its five principles are what it asks of anyone who takes care of someone else’s story.

    • Free, prior and ongoing informed consent. You are told the purpose, the risks and the benefits before your story is shared or used again, so the decision is yours.
    • Resourcing and sensitivity. Sharing a story can be hard. The people asking for it give you the practical, emotional and cultural support you need.
    • Further empowerment. You hold the decision over how your story is used, without pressure.
    • Co-design and collaboration. You stay involved in how your story is edited and framed.
    • Integrity of Story. Your story is not changed or adapted without your consent.

    Read the T.E.S.T. Principles (PDF)

  3. 3

    Check in with yourself

    Six questions drawn from Our Race’s guide for Story Holders. Tap an answer for each. Nothing here is sent or saved. It is for you.

    Is it clear why I am being asked to share my story?

    Do I feel safe and comfortable sharing it today?

    Do I know who will hear it, and whether it will be recorded?

    Have I had time to reflect on the story I want to tell?

    Do I know who I can talk to afterwards?

    Do I know what happens if I change my mind?

    Any “Not yet” or “Not sure”?

    Talk to Doug or Hawanatu before you share. You can also keep your story for another day. You decide if, when and how it is shared.
