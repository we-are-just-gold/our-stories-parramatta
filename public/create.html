Create

your own story.

Shape your own story with Our Race. You decide what you tell, and what you keep.

Create is led by Our Race

Our Race Community is an anti-racism storytelling charity that champions authentic narrative representation. It partners directly with individuals and communities affected by racial and social injustices, amplifying voices that have historically been marginalised. At the heart of its approach is the Transformational Ethical Story Telling (T.E.S.T.) Framework, placing the wellbeing, agency and safety of Story Holders at the centre of how stories are invited, developed and shared.

    First session today? Start your day

    The arrival survey. 10 short questions about you and your community, with no name.

    Start your day

  2. 2

    What happens in Create

    • Two rooms with Our Race: a storytelling workshop for adults, and a writing and performance session for ages 15 to 25.
    • Why the T.E.S.T. Framework matters. Being a story holder. Choosing what to share and what to keep.
    • Short exercises that start a story in an easy way, then space to develop your own.
    • At the end, time to send your story on the app if you want to, and to say if you would like a stage spot in Share.

    Nobody under 18 joins any session without a parent or guardian’s consent form.

  3. 3

    Your story

    Write it here if you want to, in full, with or without your name. Wrote it by hand? Take a photo of the page and send that. It does not need to be finished or tidy. You decide what you tell.

    Share your story

    Stage spots come after the circles in Share. They are photographed and filmed. Only if you want one. Your facilitators confirm the time for each spot on the day.
