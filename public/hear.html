Hear

local stories.

Local voices on what it takes to belong in Parramatta. Coming just to listen is taking part.

With you in Hear

Dr Rumbi Mabambe (Dr Ru)

Your facilitator for the day

A facilitator, researcher and CEO of Chain Reaction Foundation. She brings warmth and curiosity to conversations about what helps people feel heard, connected and at home in their communities.

DEFNE

Performing in Hear

A queer neurodivergent Turkish-Australian multidisciplinary artist and the Creative Director and Host of West Side Poetry Slam in Parramatta. Photo: Justin Cueno

  1. 1

    First session today? Start your day

    Dr Ru gives the room five minutes for this after the welcome.

    The arrival survey. 10 short questions about you and your community, with no name.

    Start your day

  2. 2

    What happens in Hear

    • 10am to 10.30am. Welcome to Country and an opening reflection, then five minutes to start your day on the app.
    • About 45 minutes. A conversation with a children’s author, who reads from his book. Spoken word from DEFNE, and a conversation with her.
    • About 45 minutes. Dr Ru’s three questions. Your answers come up on the screen and the room talks about them.
    • Doug from Our Race sets up Create before lunch.

    Hear sets the tone for the day and begins drawing out its themes: belonging, resilience, allyship and community.

  3. 3

    Dr Ru’s three questions

    Finish each sentence in your own words. Answer one, two or all three.

    No name. Your answers show on the screen in the room, so everyone can see what the room is saying. Leave out names and personal details, yours and other people’s. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. If something brings things up, the support numbers are here.

    See what the room is saying

  4. 4

    Quick fire

    Six quick ones about friends, neighbours and places. These do not go on the screen.

    I feel confident making friends

    I can speak up confidently when I disagree

    There are places nearby where I can hang out with my friends

    People in my neighbourhood help each other

    I know my neighbours and get along with them

    No name. Skip any you like.

  5. 5

    Anything else that stayed with you?

    No name. Send as many as you like.

  6. 6

    Staying on, or heading off?

    Staying. Lunch is at 12pm for everyone, and Create starts at 12.45pm.

    Go to lunch

    Leaving after Hear.

    The end of day survey. About five minutes on what you heard, who you met and what you will carry home.

    End your day
