For facilitators and hosts

What you heard

from the room.

Cues for your script, and five minutes of reflection at the end of the day. Your reflections go into the report as what our facilitators heard.

Where to send the room, session by session

One QR code opens the app. Each session has its own colour in the menu and at the top of its page, so you can say the colour.

Hear. Light blue

  • After the welcome. “Open the app and tap Start your day.” Five minutes.
  • The three questions. “Tap Hear, the light blue one, and go to step 3.” Put the live screen on the projector.
  • Quick fire. “Step 4 on the same page.” These do not show on the screen.
  • Before lunch. “If you are leaving now, tap End your day.”

Lunch. Pink

  • Going to lunch. “Tap Lunch, the pink one, for what is on and what is in the food.”

Create. Green

  • At the start. “New today? Tap Start your day first.”
  • At the end. “Tap Create, the green one. Step 3 to send your story, with or without your name. Photos of your pages are fine.”
  • Stage spots. “Step 4 to put your name down for the stage.” You can enter a name for someone.

Share. Purple

  • Before the stage. “Tap Share, the purple one. Step 3 if you would rather we read it for you.”
  • At the tables. “Step 4 for one message from your circle.”
  • Before 4.50pm. “Tap End your day before you go.”

Host controls for the live screen: open the screen with host controls and enter the host code to hide an answer.
