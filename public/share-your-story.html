Share your story

What does belonging look like

for you?

Only if you want to. Tell it in full, with your name or without it.

Stories sent without a name are used in the report without one. If you choose to be named, your story can appear in the report as a case study, named the way you chose.

Just Gold holds what you send here on behalf of SydWest. It keeps the record for one month after the day, then hands it to SydWest and deletes its own copy. Read the privacy notice.

1. Your story

It does not need to be finished or tidy. You decide what you tell, and what you keep. You can use your mother tongue.

2. Add pictures

Optional. Up to five. Photos of you, of your work, of something that carries your story, or of the pages if you wrote it by hand. A picture can show who you are even without your name, so only send ones you are happy for us to use, and ask anyone else in them first.
